VentAway Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

VentAway helps you type or say what's on your mind and release it — by shredding, drowning, burning, or otherwise letting a thought go. VentAway does not collect, store, or transmit anything you write or say. There is no account, no server, and no analytics of any kind.

Your Words Never Leave Your Device

What you type or dictate exists only in your iPhone's memory while you're using the app. It is never written to a file, never saved as a journal or history, and never sent anywhere — not to a server, not for analytics, not for any purpose. Closing the app or completing a release discards it immediately. There is nothing to delete later, because nothing was ever kept.

On-Device Dictation

If you use the microphone, VentAway uses Apple's on-device Speech framework to turn your voice into text on your iPhone. Availability depends on your language and device. Audio is not recorded to a file or kept in any form — only the transcribed text appears in the text field, and it follows the same rule above: it's never saved or sent anywhere. You can always type instead.

A Local, On-Device Safety Check

Before showing a release animation, VentAway runs a simple, local keyword check on your text to see whether it describes self-harm. This check happens entirely on your device using a fixed word list — nothing is sent anywhere to perform it, and no record of the check or its result is kept. If it matches, VentAway pauses and shows a support screen with crisis resources instead of a release animation. This is a basic safeguard, not a diagnosis or a reliable detector.

What Is Stored Locally, and Why

A small local database on your device keeps only: your app preferences (sound, haptics, Reduce Motion, default release method), and anonymous counters like how many times each release method was used and how long a session lasted. None of this includes the words you wrote or said. This local data stays on your device until you clear it in Settings or uninstall the app.

No Account, No Cloud, No Analytics

VentAway has no account system, no cloud sync, and no advertising or analytics SDKs of any kind. Nothing about your use of the app is collected, aggregated, or shared.

Purchases

Lifetime Unlock is a one-time in-app purchase processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. VentAway does not see or store your payment details; Apple handles purchases under its own privacy policy.

Not Therapy or a Crisis Service

VentAway is an expressive tool, not therapy, medical advice, or a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or someone you trust. The support screen may link to external resources (such as a crisis line), which have their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

VentAway does not collect personal information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect personal information at all.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact maxadlaistudio@gmail.com. Contacting support is optional; the email contains only what you choose to send.
