Unpixo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Unpixo is developed by MAX AI Studio. You hide a picture behind tiles and unlock the pieces by walking, running, jumping rope, doing push-ups or yoga, alone or with friends. Unpixo has no account, no sign-up, no ads and no purchases. Solo challenges are stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device only

  • Challenges and progress: the artwork you chose, your goal, number of pieces, activities you record (type, amount, start and end time), and the pictures you collect.
  • Your own photo (optional): if you choose “Use your own photo” for a solo challenge, Unpixo copies that one photo into the app’s private folder. It is never uploaded.
  • Preferences: nickname, units (km or miles) and whether you have seen the intro.

You can delete all of it with Settings › Privacy & Data › Delete All Local Data, or by deleting the app. We do not receive this data.

Location

Unpixo uses your precise location only while you are recording a walk or run that you started, to measure distance (this can continue with the screen locked; iOS shows a location indicator). Only the total distance is kept. The route and GPS points are not saved and are never uploaded or shared. Push-ups and jump rope are counted by you and are labelled self-reported. Yoga is timed on your phone. These never need location.

Group challenges (optional)

Group challenges use a server run by MAX AI Studio on Supabase. They are the only feature that sends data off your device. When you create or join a group challenge, Unpixo:

  • creates an anonymous identifier automatically (no email, phone number or Apple ID). If you delete the app or move to a new phone, access to your groups is lost;
  • sends the nickname you typed (optional), the challenge settings (artwork id, goal, pieces, deadline) and, for each activity you add, a summary contribution: the amount, the activity type and the time. No route, coordinates, photos or exact location are sent;
  • shows the other members of that challenge your nickname and your contribution total. Invitation links contain a secret token; only a hash of it is stored on the server, and a link can be replaced at any time.

You can leave a group at any time. When you leave, delete a challenge or ask us to delete your data, we remove your membership and contributions. Group features are switched off if the server is not configured.

Photos

If you choose a photo, Unpixo reads only the photo you pick. If you tap Save to Photos after finishing a challenge, it adds the picture you are saving. Sharing sends only an image you choose, through the iOS share sheet.

What we do not do

We do not show ads, do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs, do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and do not sell your data. The Message Board on the Home screen shows notices built into the app.

Children

Unpixo is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. Group challenges let people you invite see only a nickname you choose; please do not use your real full name.

Health and safety

Unpixo provides game-like tools and is not medical advice. Exercise safely and within your limits. You are responsible for your own safety, including when walking or running outdoors.

Your choices and rights

You can delete local data in the app, leave groups, and turn location off in iOS Settings. To access or delete data held on our server, or to ask any privacy question, contact support@maxaistudio.com.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above, and for material changes we will tell you in the app.
