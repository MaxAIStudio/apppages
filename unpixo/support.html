Unpixo Support

Unpixo hides a picture behind tiles. Move to unlock the pieces and collect the picture. Need help? Email support@maxaistudio.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How it works

  • Start a challenge: tap the + button, choose an activity (Walk, Run, Jump Rope, Push-ups or Yoga), a hidden picture, a goal and the number of pieces.
  • Record: open the challenge and tap Record Activity. Walks and runs are measured by GPS; Jump Rope and Push-ups are counted by you; Yoga uses a timer.
  • Unlock: every time your total passes a share of the goal, a tile flips. Finish all pieces to reveal and collect the picture.
  • One challenge, one unit: a challenge uses one measure (distance, repetitions or time). Each activity counts toward the challenge you pick.

Change the goal, pieces and deadline

On Set Up Challenge you can type or step the goal in km/miles, reps or minutes, tap a suggestion, choose 4 to 36 pieces (9 by default) and, for group challenges, set a deadline.

Group challenges

Choose Group when you create a challenge, then share the invitation link. Friends open it (or paste it under Join a Group) and add to the same picture on their own days. Group challenges need an internet connection. Your group access is tied to this install: if you delete the app or switch phones you cannot rejoin the same member slot.

Troubleshooting

  • GPS does not record: allow location in iOS Settings › Unpixo › Location (While Using the App). Step outside for a clear sky view; weak signals are shown on screen. Very short sessions (under 50 m) are not counted.
  • A recording was interrupted: if the app was closed during a yoga session, you can resume or finish with the time counted so far. Time while the app was closed is not counted.
  • Repetitions are limited: there are sensible limits per entry and per day for self-reported activities.
  • Save to Photos fails: allow adding photos in Settings › Unpixo › Photos.
  • My progress is gone: solo data is stored only on your device. Deleting the app or using Delete All Local Data removes it and it cannot be recovered.

Your data

Delete everything on your phone in Settings › Privacy & Data › Delete All Local Data. To remove your group membership or ask us to delete data on our server, email us. See the Privacy Policy.

Stay safe

Unpixo only provides tools. Exercise within your limits and pay attention to your surroundings. You are responsible for your own safety.
