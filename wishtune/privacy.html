WishTune Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2, 2026

WishTune (“we”, “our”, “us”) helps users create blessing cards and short blessing videos with optional AI-generated text/images, voice recordings, background music, dynamic effects, and profile imagery. This Privacy Policy explains what data we process and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Content you provide: recipient name, sender name, blessing text, occasion, tone, language, font, color, template, dynamic emoji, and other card settings.
  • Optional media: profile avatar, card background images, uploaded background music, and voice recordings you create in-app.
  • Generated content: generated card text, card images, video files, preview images, and share-ready media created by the app.
  • Purchase data: purchase status and transaction metadata from Apple In-App Purchase APIs (we do not receive your card number).
  • Technical data: limited diagnostics (for example, request success/failure and usage counters) for reliability and abuse prevention.

2. How We Use Information

  • Generate, preview, render, save, and share blessing cards and optional video cards.
  • Provide optional AI text/image generation.
  • Apply selected templates, fonts, dynamic emoji effects, profile avatar, voice recording, and background music.
  • Verify purchase entitlement for premium features.
  • Maintain app security, prevent abuse, and improve service quality.

2.1 In-App Purchase Notes

  • WishTune premium purchase is a one-time In-App Purchase for premium blessing card features shown in the app.
  • Payment is processed by Apple and charged to your Apple Account at purchase confirmation.
  • We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Use the app’s restore option to recover eligible purchases for the same Apple ID.

3. AI Processing

When you use AI features, your prompt content may include occasion, recipient/sender names, relationship, blessing text, style settings, and image prompt details. This content may be sent to our server gateway and then to third-party AI providers to generate results. We apply safety guardrails and may refuse unsafe requests. If you use only free templates or local video rendering, AI provider processing may not be required for that action.

4. Local Media and Device Permissions

Voice recording, profile avatar selection, uploaded images, uploaded BGM, generated previews, and video exports may be stored locally on your device or in temporary app storage so the app can preview and share your creation. The app may request access to microphone, photo library, camera, or files only when you choose related features.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We only share data with service providers necessary to operate WishTune, such as cloud hosting, analytics/logging, AI model providers, and Apple payment infrastructure.

6. Data Retention

User content and diagnostics are retained only as needed for feature delivery, legal compliance, fraud prevention, and service reliability, then deleted or anonymized where practical. Locally stored projects, profile avatar, recordings, uploaded media, and generated files remain on your device until you delete them, overwrite them, clear app data, or uninstall the app.

7. Your Choices

  • You can edit or delete local content in the app.
  • You may choose not to use voice recording, profile avatar, uploaded BGM, custom background images, or AI features.
  • You can use free templates without purchasing premium features.
  • You may contact us to request support or data-related help.

8. Children’s Privacy

WishTune is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will address the issue.

9. International Processing

Depending on infrastructure providers, data may be processed in multiple regions. We take reasonable measures to protect data in transit and at rest.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material updates will be reflected by revising the date above.
