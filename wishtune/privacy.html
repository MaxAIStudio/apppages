WishTune Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 26, 2026

WishTune (“we”, “our”, “us”) helps users create blessing cards with optional AI-generated text/images and optional voice recordings. This Privacy Policy explains what data we process and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Content you provide: recipient name, sender name, blessing text, style/occasion selections.
  • Optional media: background images you upload and voice recordings you create in-app.
  • Purchase data: purchase status and transaction metadata from Apple In-App Purchase APIs (we do not receive your card number).
  • Technical data: limited diagnostics (for example, request success/failure and usage counters) for reliability and abuse prevention.

2. How We Use Information

  • Generate and render blessing cards and optional video cards.
  • Provide optional AI text/image generation.
  • Verify purchase entitlement for premium features.
  • Maintain app security, prevent abuse, and improve service quality.

3. AI Processing

When you use AI features, your prompt content may be sent to our server gateway and then to third-party AI providers to generate results. We apply safety guardrails and may refuse unsafe requests.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We only share data with service providers necessary to operate WishTune, such as cloud hosting, analytics/logging, AI model providers, and Apple payment infrastructure.

5. Data Retention

User content and diagnostics are retained only as needed for feature delivery, legal compliance, fraud prevention, and service reliability, then deleted or anonymized where practical.

6. Your Choices

  • You can edit or delete local content in the app.
  • You may choose not to use voice recording or AI features.
  • You may contact us to request support or data-related help.

7. Children’s Privacy

WishTune is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will address the issue.

8. International Processing

Depending on infrastructure providers, data may be processed in multiple regions. We take reasonable measures to protect data in transit and at rest.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material updates will be reflected by revising the date above.

10. Contact
