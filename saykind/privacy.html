SayKind Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

SayKind is developed by MAX AI Studio. SayKind helps you say a difficult thing kindly: you describe a moment, SayKind offers a few message options to choose from, and you can turn the one you like into a shareable card. SayKind has no account and does not save your messages or cards anywhere but your own device.

Data We Store on Your Device

The moments you describe, the message options generated for them, the message you choose, any card image you save (the finished picture with your words on it), and app settings such as your language are stored only on your device, in a local app database and as local image files. None of this is uploaded to MAX AI Studio or synced to any account — there is no SayKind account to sync to. If you delete the app, this local data is deleted with it.

AI Message Generation

Turning your situation into message options is an AI feature. When you tap “Help me say it,” SayKind sends only what that step needs to our processing service to generate a result:

  • The moment you selected (for example, “apology” or “comfort”), who the message is for, and the short description you typed of what happened, plus any extra detail you added.
  • Our service forwards this to an AI model to write three message options in your chosen language, and returns them to the app.
  • MAX AI Studio does not keep this text stored long-term on its own servers once the result is returned.
  • Nothing is saved to your on-device library until you actually pick a message and choose to turn it into a card — browsing the generated options does not save anything.
  • Please avoid including anyone's full name, address, phone number, or other sensitive identifying details in what you type, since that text is sent to our AI service to generate your message.

Cards and Images

A card is the picture you create by combining a background with your message text. Cards are composed and rendered entirely on your device. When you tap Save, the finished image is written to a folder inside SayKind's own app storage and a record is added to your local library (the Saved tab) — this never leaves your device. When you tap Share, the finished image is handed to iOS's own share sheet so you can send it through whatever app you choose (Messages, another app, or saving it to Photos from there); SayKind itself does not upload or post anything on your behalf.

What We Don't Collect

SayKind does not have an account system, does not use cloud sync, does not use third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and does not track you across other apps or websites.

Data Retention

Local app data remains on your device until you delete individual saved cards, clear them from the Saved tab, or uninstall the app. There is no copy of your messages or cards on our servers to retain.

Children's Privacy

SayKind is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact maxadlaistudio@gmail.com.
