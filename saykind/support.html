SayKind Support

Last updated: September 27, 2026

SayKind helps you say a difficult thing kindly — describe a moment, pick from a few message options, and turn the one you like into a card you can share. Need help? Contact us at maxadlaistudio@gmail.com.

Common Topics

1) What SayKind Does

  • Describe the moment: pick what's going on (an apology, comfort, a boundary, reconnecting, explaining yourself, and more), who it's for, and a short description of what happened.
  • Choose a tone: SayKind offers three message options — sincere, gentle, and light — so you can pick what fits.
  • Make it a card: turn your chosen message into a picture with your words on it, ready to save or share.
  • Saved tab: a local library of every card you've saved, organized by when you made them.

2) Getting a Message

  • Tap “Help me say it” after filling in the moment, relationship, and what happened. You can add extra detail if you want a more specific result.
  • SayKind writes the three options in whichever language you have selected in the app.
  • If a result doesn't feel right, you can regenerate or adjust your description and try again.
  • You're always free to edit the text yourself before turning it into a card.

3) Making and Saving a Card

  • After picking a message, choose a background image and a text style to make your card.
  • Save keeps the finished card — the image with your text baked in, plus the date — in SayKind's own Saved tab, on your device only.
  • Share opens the iOS share sheet so you can send the finished image anywhere (Messages, another app, or save it to your Photos from there). Share does not add anything to your Saved tab.
  • You can delete any saved card from the Saved tab at any time.

4) Language

  • SayKind supports English, 中文, Español, हिन्दी, العربية, Português, and বাংলা.
  • Change your language from Settings; new messages are generated in the language you have selected.

5) Your Data

  • Everything SayKind saves — your message history and your saved cards — stays on your device. There is no account and nothing is synced to the cloud.
  • Only the short description you type is sent to our AI service to generate message options; saved cards and images are never uploaded.
  • Deleting the app deletes everything SayKind has stored, including saved cards.

6) Accuracy

  • Message suggestions are AI-generated starting points, not a guarantee of the right words for every situation — always read them over and adjust to fit your own voice before sending.

When Contacting Support

  • Device model and iOS version.
  • SayKind app version (Settings › About, if shown).
  • What happened and what you expected.
  • Screenshots and any error message.
  • Which language you had selected, if the issue is about a generated message.
