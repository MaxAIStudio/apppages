ParkTimer Support

Last updated: August 2, 2026

If you need help with ParkTimer, contact us at maxadlaistudio@gmail.com.

ParkTimer is an AI-powered Australian parking reminder app. You can use Scan Sign, Scan Ticket, or Set Timer to create a parking reminder.

Common Topics

1) What ParkTimer Does

  • Scan Sign: take a photo of an Australian parking sign and let ParkTimer interpret it with AI.
  • Scan Ticket: take a photo of a parking ticket and let ParkTimer read the expiry details.
  • Set Timer: create a parking reminder manually without AI.
  • Park History: view previous parking sessions and saved locations.
  • Plans & Pricing: manage single-scan purchases and the monthly subscription.

2) AI Scan Results Look Wrong or Incomplete

  • Use a clear photo with good lighting and minimal tilt.
  • Capture the full sign or ticket, including time ranges, exceptions, and expiry details.
  • Avoid motion blur, glare, shadows, and cropped text.
  • If needed, retake the photo from a closer distance and keep text sharp.
  • Always manually verify the physical sign or parking ticket before leaving your vehicle.

3) AI Processing Takes Time

If ParkTimer seems to pause while scanning:

  • The photo may be uploading securely for AI analysis.
  • Network quality can affect upload and response time.
  • Very complex signs or low-quality photos can take longer to interpret.
  • If the request times out or fails, try again with a clearer image.

4) Location Access and Saved Parking Location

If location is unavailable or not saved:

  • iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services: enabled.
  • iOS Settings > ParkTimer > Location: set to While Using the App.
  • Ensure GPS signal is stable. Outdoor/open sky positioning is usually better.
  • After granting permission, create or update a parking session again.

5) Reminder Notifications and Sound

If reminders are missing or delayed, please check the following:

  • iOS Settings > Notifications > ParkTimer: allow alerts, sounds, and badges.
  • In ParkTimer Settings, confirm Sound on is enabled and a notification sound is selected.
  • Confirm reminder times (first/second reminder) are set to values before leave-by time.
  • Disable Focus / Do Not Disturb temporarily to verify alert delivery.
  • On simulator, notification behavior may differ. Please verify on a real iPhone.

6) Plans, Single Scans, and Monthly Subscription

  • AI scans are paid. There is no free AI scan tier in the current model.
  • Single Scan costs AUD $0.499 for each AI sign or ticket scan, with a daily limit of up to 20 scans.
  • Monthly costs AUD $1.99 and supports normal personal use, protected by fair use limits.
  • The current fair use protection for Monthly is up to 20 AI scans per day and 500 AI scans per month.
  • Payments, renewals, and billing are handled by Apple through your App Store account.
  • Single-scan purchases are consumables and cannot be restored. If Monthly access does not update after purchase, open the pricing page and try Restore Monthly.

7) Settings Overview

  • Language: switch between English and Chinese.
  • App color: change the primary app accent color.
  • Save photos to Photos: keep a copy of parking photos in iOS Photos.
  • Plans & Pricing: buy single scans, view Monthly, and restore Monthly purchases.
  • Advanced: holiday region and parsing-related settings.
  • Reminder times: sound, notification tone, first/second reminder timing.
  • Help: About, Disclaimer, Disclaimer agreement status, Support email.

8) Advanced Settings (Holiday Region)

  • Open Settings and turn Advanced to On.
  • Enable Auto-detect Public Holiday region if you want region switching by location.
  • Or disable auto-detect and manually pick a fixed holiday region or state.
  • If a result looks wrong around public holidays, verify the selected region or state before scanning again.

When Contacting Support

Please include these details so we can help faster:

  • Device model and iOS version (for example, iPhone 13, iOS 18.x).
  • App version and build number.
  • A short description of what happened and what you expected.
  • Screenshots of relevant screens and any error message.
  • For AI scan issues: whether it was Scan Sign or Scan Ticket, and if possible, a screenshot of the result.
  • For purchase issues: Apple ID region and approximate purchase time.
