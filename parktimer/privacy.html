ParkTimer Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

ParkTimer is developed by MAX AI Studio. We designed ParkTimer to work primarily on your device, while allowing secure image upload when AI interpretation is needed.

Data Collection

ParkTimer stores parking history, reminder details, and most related app data locally on your device. When you use AI-powered features such as Scan Sign or Scan Ticket, the image you choose may be uploaded securely to the MaxAI Studio AI service for interpretation.

We do not require account registration to use ParkTimer. We do not sell your personal data.

Data Storage and Retention

Your parking records and related data remain on your device unless you choose to delete them. If you uninstall the app, locally stored app data may be removed by your operating system. If you enable saving photos to the Photos app, copies stored in Photos are managed by you.

Images submitted for AI interpretation may be processed temporarily to generate a result and improve service reliability, security, and abuse monitoring. We aim to keep this processing limited to what is reasonably necessary for the feature to work.

Permissions

  • Camera: Used to capture parking signs, parking tickets, or car photos.
  • Photo Library: Used to pick or optionally save parking-related photos.
  • Location (When In Use): Used to save your parking location and help display the parked area.
  • Notifications: Used to deliver parking reminder alerts.

In-App Purchases

ParkTimer currently includes 5 lifetime free AI scans per device for Scan Sign and Scan Ticket. After those free scans are used, continued AI scan access requires a Monthly subscription.

Monthly access is intended for normal personal use and is protected by a fair use cap, currently up to 150 AI scans per month. Payments, renewals, and billing are processed by Apple through your App Store account.

Third-Party Services

ParkTimer may rely on Apple platform services required for app functionality, such as StoreKit, local notifications, and mapping frameworks. For AI interpretation, ParkTimer uses the MaxAI Studio AI service. Payment transactions are handled by Apple according to Apple's terms and policies.

Usage Analytics

To help operate and improve ParkTimer, we may record limited usage events related to core features, including AI scan usage, manual timer usage, purchase state, and ad click activity. These records are used for service reliability, product improvement, cost control, and abuse monitoring.

Region Support

ParkTimer currently supports Australia only. Recognition results, public holiday handling, and leave-by calculations may be inaccurate outside Australia.

Children's Privacy

ParkTimer is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your Choices

You can manage app permissions (Camera, Photos, Location, Notifications) at any time in iOS Settings. You can also delete parking records and photos from your device.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact maxadlaistudio@gmail.com.
