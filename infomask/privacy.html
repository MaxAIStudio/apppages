InfoMask Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

InfoMask is developed by MAX AI Studio. InfoMask helps you hide private details — faces, text, and other sensitive information — in screenshots and photos before you share them. InfoMask is on-device first: detection and masking happen entirely on your iPhone.

On-Device Processing

InfoMask uses Apple's on-device Vision framework to detect faces, text, and barcodes in the photos you choose. This detection, and the masking you apply, happens entirely on your device. Your original photos and the screenshots you redact are never uploaded to MAX AI Studio or any third party, and InfoMask has no account and no cloud sync.

Data We Store on Your Device

Your app settings (auto-detection, default mask style, history opt-in), and — only if you turn on "Save redacted copies in history" — copies of the redacted images you export, are stored locally in an app database and as local files. Original, unredacted photos are never modified or replaced. History entries stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app, and the app's local storage is excluded from iCloud and device backups.

Photos and Camera Access

  • Photos: to let you choose a screenshot or photo to redact, and to check for a recent screenshot to suggest on Home. InfoMask only reads the specific image you pick (or, for the Home suggestion, the most recent screenshot) — it does not scan or upload your photo library.
  • Camera: only if you choose "Take Photo & Mask" or "Take Photos & Mask", to capture a new photo to redact.

In-App Messages and Promotions

InfoMask may contact MAX AI Studio's servers to fetch an optional home-screen message or a rotating promotional banner for other MAX AI Studio apps. These requests never include your photos, screenshots, or redacted images — only basic, non-personal app identifiers needed to serve the right content.

Share Extension

InfoMask's iOS Share Extension lets you redact a screenshot directly from the Photos app or another app's share sheet, using the same on-device detection and masking. Processing still happens entirely on your device.

Data Retention

Local app data, including any saved history entries, remains on your device until you delete them individually, clear your history, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling InfoMask removes all locally stored data.

Accuracy Disclaimer

InfoMask's detection is a suggestion, not a guarantee. It can miss details or mask the wrong area. Always review a redacted copy carefully before sharing or saving it — you are responsible for confirming that everything you intend to hide is actually hidden.

Children's Privacy

InfoMask is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact
