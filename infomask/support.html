InfoMask Support

Last updated: September 17, 2026

InfoMask helps you hide private details — faces, text, and other sensitive information — in screenshots and photos before you share them, entirely on your iPhone. Need help? Contact us at maxadlaistudio@gmail.com.

Common Topics

1) Redacting a Screenshot

  • Redact Latest Screenshot on Home opens your most recent screenshot directly, if InfoMask already has permission to see it.
  • Choose Screenshot opens the system photo picker so you can pick any photo.
  • Take Photo & Mask opens the camera to capture a new photo to redact.
  • InfoMask suggests masks automatically (faces, text, and other detected details); tap any suggestion to toggle it, drag to move or resize it, or draw your own rectangle.

2) Batch Redacting Multiple Screenshots

  • On the Batch tab, Choose Screenshots lets you select multiple images from your library at once (up to 20).
  • Take Photos & Mask lets you capture several photos in a row with the camera, reviewing each shot before moving to the next.
  • Presets like "Hide Everyone" or "Sensitive Only" apply across every selected image at once.

3) Mask Styles

  • Choose solid, pixelate, or blur for any individual mask, or set a default style in Settings.
  • Detected faces default to a smile-style cover; you can change any face's style individually in the Faces panel.

4) History

  • Turn on Save redacted copies in history in Settings to keep a local copy of every export, organised by year and month.
  • Tap a year or month header to collapse or expand that section.
  • Tap an entry's … menu to re-share, preview, or delete it.
  • History is off by default and stored only on your device — see the Privacy Policy for details.

5) Share Extension

  • From the Photos app (or any app's share sheet), tap Share, then choose InfoMask to redact a screenshot without leaving that app.
  • If InfoMask isn't listed, tap More in the share sheet and enable it.

6) Accuracy

  • Detection is a suggestion, not a guarantee — it can miss details or mask the wrong area.
  • Always review the exported/redacted copy carefully before sharing or saving it.

When Contacting Support

InfoMask does not automatically send diagnostic information or images. Contacting support is optional. Share only the details you are comfortable providing.

  • Device model and iOS version.
  • What happened and what you expected.
  • A redacted screenshot of the issue, if helpful. Hide private details before sending.
  • Whether it happened in the main app or the Share Extension.
