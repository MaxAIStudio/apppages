Dueora Help

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Dueora helps you scan bills, receipts, warranties, IDs and other documents, find important dates, and get reminded before they are due or expire. Need help? Contact us at maxadlaistudio@gmail.com.

Common Topics

1) Scanning Documents

  • Use the plus button to take a photo, choose a photo, or upload a PDF/document.
  • When you use AI scanning, the selected image or PDF page is sent to OpenAI for AI-assisted extraction.
  • For PDFs with multiple pages, Dueora asks you to choose one page to scan at a time.
  • After AI extraction, review every field against the original document before saving.
  • If Dueora cannot find a due date or expiry date, enter the date manually before saving.

2) Supported Items

  • Bills, receipts, warranties, subscriptions, renewals, return windows, IDs, registrations, insurance documents and more.
  • You can also add items manually and attach photos, screenshots, PDFs or other files.
  • Categories can be selected or customized when editing an item.

3) Reminders and Notifications

  • Enable notifications in iOS Settings so Dueora can deliver reminder alerts.
  • If a reminder date would be in the past, Dueora automatically adjusts it to the due date.
  • If alerts do not appear, check iOS notification permission, Focus mode, Do Not Disturb and device time settings.

4) Calendar, Sharing and Attachments

  • You can add saved items to iPhone Calendar from the detail page.
  • You can share reminder details using the iOS Share Sheet.
  • Attachments stay on your device as local files unless you choose to share or export them.

5) Backup and Restore

  • Dueora supports JSON backup export through the iOS Share Sheet.
  • You can import a Dueora JSON backup from this device.
  • Import checks for likely duplicates and lets you merge or replace after confirmation.

6) Dueora Pro Subscription

  • Dueora Pro is a yearly subscription for AI-powered scanning.
  • The App Store product ID is dueora_pro.
  • Pricing, storefront currency, renewal timing and billing are handled by Apple and shown in the App Store purchase sheet.
  • Dueora Pro includes up to 500 AI scans per subscription year.
  • Tap Restore to recover an active subscription purchased with the same Apple ID.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription from Apple account subscription settings.

7) AI Accuracy

  • AI extraction can be incomplete, inaccurate, or misread.
  • You must review the original document and confirm due dates, expiry dates, account numbers, amounts, notes, and reminders before saving or acting on them.
  • Dueora is a reminder tool only. You remain responsible for tracking bills, renewals, warranties, expiry dates, deadlines, and other obligations.
  • Do not rely solely on Dueora or AI output for legal, financial, medical, safety-critical or time-critical decisions.

When Contacting Support

  • Device model and iOS version.
  • Dueora app version/build number.
  • What happened and what you expected.
  • Screenshots, error messages, and whether the issue happened during photo, PDF, manual entry, import, notification, purchase or restore.
