Dueora Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 22, 2026

Dueora is developed by MAX AI Studio. Dueora is designed to help you capture important dates from documents and manage reminders with a local-first approach.

Data We Store

Saved items, important dates, categories, notes, reminder settings, confirmation timestamps, and attachment references are stored locally on your device using an app database. Attachments such as photos and PDFs remain as local files unless you choose to share, export, or submit them for AI scanning.

AI Scanning

When you start AI-powered scanning, Dueora may securely send the selected photo, screenshot, PDF page image, document content, filename, and related metadata to MAX AI Studio services and AI providers such as OpenAI to extract dates and structured fields.

  • For multi-page PDFs, Dueora asks you to choose one page and scans only that page.
  • AI results are suggestions. You should review and confirm details before saving.
  • Dueora records AI usage events and app usage events for debugging, abuse prevention, subscription enforcement, quota tracking, and product analytics.

Usage Logs and Analytics

Dueora may send usage events such as scan started, scan completed, import/export actions, subscription actions, errors, and anonymized install identifiers to MAX AI Studio services. These logs help us operate the app, understand failures, enforce scan limits, and improve reliability.

Subscription and Billing

  • Dueora Pro is a yearly auto-renewing subscription for AI-powered scanning.
  • The App Store product ID is dueore_pro.
  • Dueora Pro includes up to 500 AI scans per subscription year.
  • Pricing, currency, billing, renewal, restore, cancellation, and subscription management are handled by Apple through your App Store account.
  • Dueora verifies purchase status with MAX AI Studio services to enable subscription access and enforce scan limits.

Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos of bills, receipts and documents.
  • Photos/Files: to import images, screenshots, PDFs and attachments.
  • Notifications: to deliver due date and expiry reminders.
  • Calendar (optional): when you choose to add a saved item to iPhone Calendar.

Backup, Import and Sharing

Dueora can export your saved data as a JSON backup through the iOS Share Sheet. You can also import a Dueora JSON backup from your device. When you share a reminder or backup, the destination app receives the content you choose to share.

Data Retention

Local app data remains on your device until you delete items, delete backups, or uninstall the app. Server-side operational logs may be retained as needed for security, diagnostics, subscription verification, quota tracking, and legal compliance.

Accuracy and Safety Disclaimer

AI extraction can be incomplete or inaccurate. Always verify important dates and details yourself. Do not rely solely on Dueora for legal, medical, financial, safety-critical, or time-critical decisions.

Children's Privacy

Dueora is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact maxadlaistudio@gmail.com.
