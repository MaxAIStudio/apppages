PaceCanvas Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

PaceCanvas is developed by MAX AI Studio. It records walks and runs with GPS and turns routes into shareable art. PaceCanvas is local-first: your routes and statistics are stored on your device, there is no account and no cloud sync.

Your privacy choice

The first time you open PaceCanvas you are asked to Accept or Decline this privacy notice. If you decline, recording, running together and sharing stay switched off until you accept. You can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy choice. Your answer, the time you gave it and a short history of changes are stored only on your device.

Data stored on your device only

  • Activities and routes: start and end time, distance, duration, and the GPS points of each recording (time, latitude, longitude, accuracy, speed, altitude).
  • Artwork settings: style, background, format, titles and privacy options for artwork you save.
  • Profile (optional): the name and photo you enter in Settings. They are shown only on images you choose to share.
  • Preferences: units, theme color, appearance, defaults, and your privacy choice.
  • Replay videos: the short route-drawing video is made on your device from your route. It is saved or shared only if you choose.

You can delete everything with Settings › Delete All Local Data, or by deleting the app. We do not receive this data.

Location

PaceCanvas uses your precise location only while you are recording an activity you started, including with the screen locked (iOS shows a location indicator). It does not collect location at any other time. Your GPS points are never uploaded to us.

Photos and camera

If you choose a profile photo, PaceCanvas reads only the photo you select or take. If you choose Save to Photos, it adds the image you export. These stay on your device.

Information sent to MAX AI Studio

  • App check-in: when you open the app it sends a random install ID (not linked to your name), app version and build, device type, iOS version, language and time zone. Our server may derive an approximate country/region/city from the IP address. We use this to count installs and active use and to show an occasional notice at the top of the Canvas screen.
  • Notice events: whether the notice was shown, tapped or dismissed.
  • Promotion panel: the bottom panel loads promotions for MAX AI Studio’s own apps. If you tap one we log the promotion, the app, the time and an IP-derived approximate location.

None of this includes your name, photo, routes or GPS points. We do not show third-party ads, do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites, and do not sell your data.

Microphone and speech (optional)

If you tap the microphone button to dictate a message in a run together, PaceCanvas asks for microphone and speech-recognition permission. Dictation is handled by Apple’s speech recognition on iOS; PaceCanvas does not receive or store the audio, only the text you send.

Running together (optional)

If you start or join a run together, PaceCanvas sends MAX AI Studio the name you type, your small profile picture (only if you have set one in Settings) and, over a live connection, the shape of your route as you move (distances relative to where you started, never latitude or longitude). The people in the same run can see your name, picture and route shape, and anyone with the run’s link can watch it in a browser. The run and its pictures are deleted from our servers a day after it ends. Chat messages (text and emoji, up to 80 characters) are relayed live to the people in the run, including people who are only watching; we do not keep a message history. If you choose Show my area on the map, a rough area rounded to about 50 km (never your exact position) is shared with the people in the run until you hide it. When you stand still, only the number of seconds you have been still is shared, so friends can see your dot grow. On your own phone the run is saved as a group activity with the names and pictures of the people who ran with you; deleting the activity or all local data removes them.

PaceCanvas Plus subscription (optional)

PaceCanvas Plus is an optional yearly auto-renewing subscription sold through the App Store. Payment, your Apple ID and your billing details are handled entirely by Apple; we never see them. To confirm that your subscription is active, the app sends the App Store receipt to MAX AI Studio’s server, which checks it with Apple and answers whether Plus is active and until when. We do not keep the receipt. A short signed token is stored on your device so the server can tell that a group is being started by a Plus subscriber. Joining or watching a run together never needs Plus. You can manage or cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings.

Sharing

Images are shared only when you use the system share sheet or Save to Photos, and only with the app or person you pick. Exported images contain no GPS metadata, but the route shape is visible; use Hide Start / Hide End to protect places such as your home.

Children

PaceCanvas is not directed to children under 13.

Changes
