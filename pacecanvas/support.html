PaceCanvas Support

Last updated: November 21, 2026

PaceCanvas records your walks and runs with GPS and turns each route into flowing line art you can share. Need help? Contact us at support@maxaistudio.com.

Stay Safe

Walking and running with a phone can be dangerous. Keep your eyes on the road, traffic and your surroundings. Do not read or type messages while you move, and stop somewhere safe before you use the phone. Follow traffic rules and local laws, and choose routes and times that are safe for you. PaceCanvas only provides tools. You are responsible for your own safety and for the people with you, and PaceCanvas is not responsible for accidents, injuries, losses or damage.

Before you can send messages or emoji in a Group Walk / Run, the app asks you to Accept or Reject this safety notice, once for each group walk or run. If you reject it, messages and emoji stay off for that group. Your answer is kept on your phone and recorded with a random install ID (not linked to your name); you can review it any time in Settings › Privacy & Data › Messaging safety.

Common Topics

1) Recording a walk or run

  • On the Go tab choose Walking or Running and tap Single Walk (or Single Run). Allow precise location when asked. The first time you open the app you are asked to accept the privacy notice; recording, group walks/runs and sharing are off until you accept (change it any time in Settings › Privacy choice).
  • You can pause, resume and finish. Tracking stops immediately when you finish.
  • Recording continues with the screen locked. A blue location indicator appears while recording, and the Lock Screen / Dynamic Island shows your route picture, time, distance and pace (iOS 16.2 or later; Live Activities must be allowed in Settings).
  • If the app is closed unexpectedly, your recorded points are saved and recovered the next time you open PaceCanvas. If you force-quit the app, iOS stops background tracking.

1b) Group Walk / Group Run (with friends)

  • Tap Group Walk (or Group Run). The sheet has two tabs: Start to open a room, and Join to join or watch with a code or link. Starting a group needs PaceCanvas Plus; joining or watching is always free.
  • Start now or schedule it. On the Start tab choose Start now, or Schedule and pick a date and time (from 5 minutes to 30 days ahead). The invitation says when it starts and when it ends (with the time zone). Every group has an end time, one hour after the start unless you change it; the group closes by itself then. A scheduled group cannot be started before its time.
  • Scheduled groups. While something is planned, a small alarm clock appears at the top right of Home (it turns green when the time has come). Tap it to open the group and press Start; the same page has Add to calendar (with a 15-minute reminder) and Send invitation. All planned groups are also listed under Activity › Scheduled; a group leaves the list when it starts. Friends who open your link before the start can Add to calendar on the invitation page and reply I’m in, Not sure or Can’t make it. You see the totals and each person’s reply under Replies on the scheduled group’s page. Reminders only show inside the app for now.
  • Share the invite (link and code) with friends. They can join as a runner or choose Just watch; people can also watch in a browser from the link.
  • You see each other’s route art grow live. Only the shape of the route is shared, never your latitude or longitude. Each person has their own colour on the final picture.
  • Chat: send text and emoji (or dictate with the microphone) to everyone in the run. Messages stay on screen until the run ends. The first time, you must accept the safety notice (see Stay Safe above); please never read or type while you move.
  • Map: switch with the blue Map / Art button. Locations appear only for people who tap Show my area, and are rough (about 50 km).
  • If connecting takes longer than about 15 seconds the app stops and asks you to try again; Cancel always brings you straight back to Home.
  • The host can end the run for everyone; anyone else can leave at any time.
  • People who only watched are listed as “Watched by” on the group card and the replay video, and under Watched on the activity page.

2) Distance, pace and GPS accuracy

  • Poor GPS samples, impossible jumps and standing-still jitter are filtered out. Pauses and signal gaps start a new segment, so no straight line is drawn across them.
  • Elapsed is recording time excluding pauses; Moving is time actually moving. Pace shows “—” until there is enough distance.
  • For best results record outdoors with a clear view of the sky.
  • Indoors or standing still: your dot grows while you stand, and turning on the spot draws scribbles, so the picture is never empty.

3) Art styles

  • Flow, Neon, Ink, Threads, Glow and Pencil. Flow and Ink are free; the others need PaceCanvas Plus. Choose them in the Studio.

4) Sharing and saving

  • Export as Post 4:5 (1080×1350), Square 1:1 (1080×1080) or Story 9:16 (1080×1920). Use Share, Save to Photos or Save Artwork. Saved and exported artwork appears under Artworks on the Activity tab.
  • Exported images contain no GPS metadata. Open Options in the Studio to change source, format, text and privacy.
  • Your name and photo (Settings › Profile) can appear on shared images; switch it off per image with Name & Photo. Images show the start and end time of the activity; the watermark can be removed with Plus.
  • Replay Video (Plus): in the Studio, tap Replay Video to make a short 9:16 video of your route being drawn stroke by stroke, then share it or save it to Photos.
  • After a workout a small cheer picture pops up for a few seconds. Turn it off in Settings › After a Workout › Show Cheer Picture.

5) Privacy controls

  • Hide Start / Hide End removes the beginning or end of the route (within the radius you choose) from previews and exports. Your saved route is never changed.
  • Settings › Delete All Local Data removes every activity, route and artwork from your device.

6) PaceCanvas Plus and cost

Recording, history, the Flow and Ink styles, sharing, and joining or watching group walks and runs are free. PaceCanvas Plus is an optional yearly auto-renewing subscription (the price is shown in the App Store in your currency) that adds: starting group walks and runs, all art styles, sharing without the watermark, and Replay Video.

  • Subscribe in Settings › PaceCanvas Plus. Payment is charged to your Apple ID and renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends.
  • Manage or cancel in your Apple ID settings (the Manage button opens it). New phone or reinstalled? Tap Restore on the same screen.
  • The panel at the bottom of the Go tab shows Max AI Studio’s own apps.
