Common Topics

1) What UnisonDay Does

  • Today: one card — a short quote over a background image — shown to everyone on the same date.
  • World: how many people have opened and shared today's card, plus a country-level breakdown.
  • My Cards: the cards you've shared or saved, stored on your device.

2) Sharing a Card

  • Tap Share This Card on the Today tab. UnisonDay renders the card as an image and opens the standard iOS share sheet — you choose where it goes.
  • Sharing adds one to today's share count for your country on the World tab.

3) Saving a Card

  • Tap the heart on a card to save it. Saved cards appear under My Cards › Saved.
  • Cards you've shared appear under My Cards › Shared.

4) The World Tab

  • The map and counts are network-enhanced: if they can't load, the Today card still works normally offline.
  • Country is determined by your network connection, never precise location or GPS.

5) Your Data

  • There is no account. Your history of seen/shared/saved cards is stored only on your device.
  • UnisonDay uses an anonymous, random install identifier to avoid counting the same device twice in a day — see the Privacy Policy for details.

When Contacting Support

  • Device model and iOS version.
  • UnisonDay app version and build number.
  • What happened and what you expected.
  • Screenshots and any error message.
