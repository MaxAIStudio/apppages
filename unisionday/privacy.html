UnisonDay Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 12, 2026

UnisonDay is developed by MAX AI Studio. UnisonDay shows everyone in the world the same card on the same day — a short quote over a background image. There are no accounts, no profiles, and no personal information is required to use the app.

Data We Store on Your Device

The days you've opened, the cards you've liked or saved, and whether you've shared today's card are stored only on your device in a local app database. This history is never uploaded and is not linked to any account, because UnisonDay has no accounts.

Anonymous Install Identifier

On first launch, UnisonDay generates a random identifier and stores it on your device. It is not linked to your name, Apple ID, or any other personal information. It is used only so our server can count how many distinct devices opened today's card without counting the same device twice, and to show your country's participation on the World tab.

What Our Server Receives

To show live totals ("N people have opened today's card," the World map, and share counts), the app sends the anonymous install identifier and today's date to the MaxAIStudio server when you open the app and when you share. The server determines your country only from your network connection at that moment — never precise location, never GPS, never city-level location. The app never connects directly to any database; it only ever talks to the MaxAIStudio API.

  • We do not collect your name, email, contacts, or any other personal information.
  • We do not use GPS or ask for location permission.
  • We do not run any third-party advertising SDK, and there is no cross-app tracking or IDFA.

Sharing a Card

When you tap "Share This Card," UnisonDay renders an image on your device and hands it to Apple's standard share sheet. UnisonDay does not post anything on your behalf — you choose where the image goes. Sharing also tells our server to increment today's share count for your country, using the same anonymous identifier described above.

Content

The daily card's text and background image are prepared in advance by MAX AI Studio and delivered through the MaxAIStudio API, with a bundled copy on your device so the app still works offline. This content contains no advertising and no user-generated material.

Data Retention

Your on-device history stays until you delete the app. Aggregate, anonymous daily counts (views and shares per country) are retained on our server to keep the World tab's historical totals accurate; these counts are never traceable back to an individual device or person.

Children's Privacy

UnisonDay is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated date.
