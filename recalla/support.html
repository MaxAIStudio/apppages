Recalla Support

Last updated: May 19, 2026

Recalla helps you remember the past, organize the present, and plan the future. Need help? Contact us at maxadlaistudio@gmail.com.

Common Topics

1) Save Mode vs Chat Mode

  • Save Mode: your input is parsed into structured records (trip, reminder, note, etc.).
  • Chat Mode: conversational responses only, no new memory record is created.
  • If you do not want auto-saving, switch to Chat Mode before sending.

2) AI Mode, BYO Token, and Paid Subscription

  • Local Mode works without API keys, but extraction quality may be simpler.
  • Bring Your Own Token: use your own provider/API key (OpenAI/Gemini/DeepSeek/OpenRouter). Provider fees are billed by that provider.
  • Paid Subscription: unlock Recalla Pro AI service in-app (Monthly/Yearly), with platform-managed model fallback.
  • You can configure or switch modes in Settings > AI Assistant.

3) Subscription, Restore, Upgrade and Manage Plan

  • Available plans: Monthly and Yearly (storefront price/currency may vary by region).
  • Tap Restore to recover an existing purchase on the same Apple account.
  • Upgrade/downgrade/cancel is handled by Apple via Manage Plan (Apple Subscriptions page).
  • When switching plans, effective time can be immediate or next renewal cycle based on Apple's subscription policy.

4) Voice Input and Language Recognition

  • Grant microphone permission in iOS Settings for Recalla.
  • For mixed-language speech, speak slightly slower and avoid overlapping phrases.
  • If recognition quality drops, stop and restart recording once.

5) Trip Plan Generation

  • Generate Plan uses a structured template to output readable travel content.
  • You can edit and save the generated plan in detail pages.
  • If AI is unavailable, local fallback may return shorter/less detailed plans.

6) Reminders, Alarm and Notifications

  • Enable notifications in iOS Settings to receive reminders and alarm alerts.
  • Alarm records can be created by chat/voice or from quick action icons.
  • If alerts do not fire, verify Focus/Do Not Disturb and notification permission.

7) Attachments and Media

  • You can attach images and supported files to memory details.
  • Image text parsing can be toggled on/off before sending.
  • If parsing is off, files are still saved as attachments.

8) Data and Safety Notes

  • Most data is stored on your device locally.
  • AI-generated content may be inaccurate. Please verify important facts and dates yourself.
  • Do not rely solely on generated output for legal, medical, financial, or safety-critical decisions.

When Contacting Support

  • Device model and iOS version.
  • App version/build number.
  • What happened vs expected behavior.
  • Screenshots and the exact error text (if any).
  • If AI-related: provider, model name, and whether test connection passed.
