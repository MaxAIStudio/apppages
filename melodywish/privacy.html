MelodyWish Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 18, 2026

MelodyWish helps you create personalized songs and lyric videos for special people and moments. We aim to keep data collection minimal. We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use third-party advertising trackers to build cross-app or cross-website profiles about you.

1. What We Collect

MelodyWish does not require a normal account to create songs or videos. Most of your project content, such as occasion settings, names, lyrics, photos, generated audio, and rendered videos, is stored locally on your device so the app can function properly.

2. Local Content Stored on Your Device

The app may store drafts, generated lyrics, downloaded song files, rendered lyric videos, and related project metadata locally on your device or in temporary app storage. This allows you to continue editing, previewing, and exporting your creations later. We do not provide a remote backup service for this local content.

3. When Data Leaves Your Device

Data may leave your device only in limited situations needed for specific features:

  • AI song and lyric generation: when you choose AI features, the text and settings needed for that request may be sent for processing so MelodyWish can return lyrics, a song, or related results.
  • Video creation assets: if a feature needs uploaded media, selected photos or related content may be processed so your requested video can be generated.
  • In-App Purchase: Apple handles payment, transaction processing, and purchase validation for paid song creation.
  • Support email: if you contact us, we receive the information you include in your email.
  • Sharing: when you share a file, that file is sent through the sharing method or app you selected.

4. AI Processing

When you actively use AI features, MelodyWish may send content such as occasion, language, song title, recipient name, sender name, style selections, lyrics brief, and lyrics text for processing. This processing is used to generate the song or lyric result you requested. If you do not use AI creation features, this processing does not occur.

5. Photos, Microphone, and Speech-Related Permissions

MelodyWish may request access to your photo library so you can select photos for lyric videos and save finished videos. The app may also request microphone or speech-related permissions for present or future media features. You can control these permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Payments

Paid song creation is handled through Apple In-App Purchase. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple may provide transaction status or limited purchase information needed to confirm payment and unlock paid song creation.

7. Usage and Diagnostic Logging

MelodyWish may send limited usage or event logs needed to operate the app, improve reliability, understand feature usage, or help investigate failures. These logs are intended to be operational in nature and may include app version, language, selected flow type, timestamps, or error context related to song or video generation.

8. No Sale of Personal Data

We do not sell personal information. We do not use advertising SDKs to track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

9. Data Retention

Because most project content is stored locally on your device, you control how long it stays there. You can delete songs, videos, and other local content from within the app, from your device, or by uninstalling MelodyWish. If you email support, we may keep that correspondence for as long as reasonably needed to respond and maintain support records.

10. Your Choices

  • You can choose not to use AI generation features.
  • You can choose not to upload photos for videos.
  • You can choose whether or not to purchase paid song creation.
  • You can choose whether or not to save or share generated files.
  • You can manage photo, microphone, and speech permissions in iOS Settings.

11. Children’s Privacy

MelodyWish is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through support or another direct contact method, please contact us and we will address it.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the date shown at the top of this page.
