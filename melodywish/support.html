MelodyWish Support

Last updated: June 18, 2026

Need help with MelodyWish? Contact us at maxadlaistudio@gmail.com. We aim to respond as quickly as practical and appreciate as much detail as you can provide.

Official support page URL: https://maxaistudio.github.io/apppages/melodywish/support.html

For privacy-related questions, please also review our Privacy Policy.

Common Topics

1) Paid song creation or restore issues

Include your App Store region, app version, screenshot of the purchase screen, and any visible error message.

MelodyWish song creation uses Apple In-App Purchase. It is not an auto-renewing subscription.

2) AI lyrics generation failed

Tell us whether you used AI Write, your selected occasion, language, style settings, and the exact error shown on screen.

AI generation may fail because of network issues, provider availability, safety filters, or unsupported prompt content.

3) Song generation failed after payment

Include the song title, selected occasion, approximate time, and screenshot of the failure message.

If payment succeeded but song generation did not complete, mention that clearly so support can review whether the same paid attempt should remain eligible for retry.

4) Video creation failed

Share your iPhone model, iOS version, selected song, subtitle option, aspect ratio, and how many photos you used.

If export or rendering fails, screenshots or a short screen recording are very helpful.

5) Photo library, save, or permission issues

Check photo library permissions in iOS Settings if import or save does not work.

Please tell us whether the problem happened while selecting photos, saving a video, or opening a generated file.

6) Audio, lyric timing, or playback issues

If song playback, lyric sync, or subtitle timing looks wrong, include the affected song title and a screen recording if possible.

This helps us tell whether the issue is in generation, timeline alignment, local playback, or video rendering.

7) Privacy help

MelodyWish mainly stores project content locally on your device and only sends data out when a specific feature needs it, such as AI generation, purchase handling, support contact, or sharing.

If you have a privacy question, email us with the subject line: "MelodyWish Privacy Request".

When contacting support, please include

  • App version
  • Device model and iOS version
  • Screenshots or screen recording
  • The exact step where the issue happened: occasion, details, style, lyrics, song creation, or video creation
  • Approximate time when the issue occurred
  • Whether payment had already succeeded before the issue appeared
