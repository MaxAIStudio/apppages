Mendora Support

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Mendora keeps a lifecycle record for your home appliances and gives you a cautious first read when something breaks. Need help? Contact us at maxadlaistudio@gmail.com.

Common Topics

1) What Mendora Does

  • Add appliances: a photo, brand, model, serial, purchase date, warranty and location. Purchase date defaults to today and warranty to one year — adjust as needed.
  • Start an issue: photograph the fault, let AI identify the appliance and return a preliminary assessment — likely causes, safe things to try, what to avoid, and an urgency level.
  • Repair flow: a five-step wizard — Identify → Assess → Prepare a repair pack → Find a repairer → Share.
  • Log Service: book a repair visit, log a completed repair with its invoice, record maintenance, or set a reminder.
  • Contacts: your saved repair companies, grouped by trade (plumber, electrician, appliance repair, and more).
  • Reminders: warranty expiries, maintenance due dates and booked repair visits, plus a bell on the Home screen for what is due soon.
  • History: every issue, repair, service and booking on one timeline per appliance.

2) The AI Preliminary Assessment

  • Write a clear description of the problem and add the error code shown on the appliance, if any.
  • The assessment is preliminary guidance only, not a professional diagnosis. Read it, do not rely on it.
  • If Mendora shows a safety warning — or you notice a gas smell, sparks, burning, smoke or water damage — stop and contact a qualified professional or emergency services.
  • You can mark an assessment helpful or not helpful; this is stored on your device and helps us improve.

3) The Repair Pack and Finding a Repairer

  • The repair pack is a plain-language message to a repairer, built from the appliance details, the problem, and the AI assessment. Edit it on the Prepare step; add your name in Settings so it signs off.
  • On the Find a repairer step you can: let Mendora search the web for nearby companies, pick one of your saved contacts, or type an email or phone number.
  • The web search uses an approximate location or a suburb / postcode you type, and runs at most twice per issue (it has a real cost). After that the button stays visible but disabled.
  • On the Share step, the pack is sent through the iOS Share Sheet with the fault photos as images. If the repairer has an email or phone, there are one-tap Email / Text shortcuts (text only).
  • Any step also has an “I fixed it myself” link to close the issue.

4) Booking a Visit and Logging the Repair

  • Log Service › Repair › Book visit: save the company and the appointment date & time. Mendora adds a reminder and shows the visit on the appliance and the Home screen.
  • When the visit is done, open that reminder and tap Log the repair, or use Log Service › Repair › Repair done. Scan the invoice to fill fields, add the cost, warranty months and a next-service date.
  • Logging a repair from an issue resolves that issue automatically.

5) Reminders and Notifications

  • Enable notifications in iOS Settings so Mendora can deliver alerts.
  • The Home-screen bell shows everything due inside your alert window. Change the window in Settings › Notifications › Alert me ahead (default 7 days).
  • Tapping a reminder notification opens the related appliance or schedule.
  • If alerts do not appear, check iOS notification permission, Focus mode, Do Not Disturb, and device time settings.

6) Contacts

  • Repairers are saved automatically when you confirm a repair, and you can add them by hand.
  • Set a Type (plumber, electrician, etc.) for the right icon; the Contacts tab shows a filter bar once you have more than one trade.
  • “Scan a business card” fills the fields with AI — check every field afterwards.

7) Scanning an Invoice or Document Looks Wrong

  • Use a clear, straight-on photo with good lighting and no glare.
  • Capture the whole document, including totals, dates and warranty terms.
  • Review every extracted field against the original before you confirm.
  • If the scan fails, the photo is still saved — fill the fields in by hand or try a sharper photo.

8) Mendora Pro and AI Actions

  • Mendora Pro is a yearly subscription that unlocks the AI features. The App Store product ID is mendora_year_pro.
  • Pricing, storefront currency, renewal timing and billing are handled by Apple and shown in the App Store purchase sheet.
  • Mendora Pro includes up to 200 AI actions per subscription period. Identifying an appliance, a preliminary assessment, a document or card scan, and a repairer search each count as one. Settings shows your usage and reset date.
  • Tap Restore Purchases to recover an active subscription bought with the same Apple ID.
  • Manage or cancel your subscription from your Apple account subscription settings.

9) Backup, Export and Sharing

  • Settings › Backup & Restore exports your data as a backup file through the iOS Share Sheet, including attached photos and documents.
  • You can import a Mendora backup from this device; Mendora checks for likely duplicates and lets you merge or replace.
  • Sharing a repair pack sends it to the app you choose.

10) Storage and Theme Colour

  • Settings › Storage shows how much space photos and documents use and can remove files no record points at.
  • Settings › Theme colour changes the app's accent colour (default teal).

11) AI Accuracy and Safety

  • AI output can be incomplete, inaccurate or misread.
  • The assessment is not a professional diagnosis. Always verify the appliance, the documents and the safety of any work.
  • Do not attempt repairs you are not qualified and licensed to do — especially anything involving gas, electricity, pressurised water or heat.
  • Do not rely solely on Mendora or AI output for safety-critical, financial or legal decisions.

When Contacting Support

  • Device model and iOS version.
  • Mendora app version and build number (Settings › About).
  • What happened and what you expected.
  • Screenshots and any error message.
  • If AI-related: whether it happened during identify, assessment, invoice / card scan, or repairer search.
  • For purchase issues: Apple ID region and approximate purchase time.
