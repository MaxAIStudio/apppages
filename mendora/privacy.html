Mendora Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

Mendora is developed by MAX AI Studio. Mendora helps you keep a lifecycle record for your home appliances — details, warranties, issues, repairs, maintenance and reminders — using a local-first approach. Your records are stored on your device by default and Mendora does not require a cloud account for normal use.

Data We Store

Appliances, issues you report, AI preliminary assessments, repair packs, logged repairs, repair contacts (name, phone, email, website, address, trade), warranties, maintenance records and reminder schedules, chosen repairers, feedback you give on AI results, and app settings are stored locally on your device in an app database. Photos and PDFs you add (appliance photos, fault photos, receipts, invoices, service reports, business cards) remain as local files on your device unless you choose to share, export, or submit them for AI processing.

AI Features

When you use an AI feature, Mendora securely sends the content that feature needs to our processing service and to OpenAI to return a result. This can include:

  • Identify an appliance: the appliance photo you take.
  • Preliminary issue assessment: the appliance details, your written description of the problem, and any error code.
  • Scan a repair invoice, receipt or service report: the selected photo or PDF page image and its extracted content.
  • Scan a business card: the selected photo of the card.
  • Find a repairer near you: an appliance category and brand, plus either a coarse (rounded) approximate location or the suburb / postcode you type. Mendora runs this web search at most twice per issue.
  • Images are downscaled before upload to reduce what is sent.
  • AI results can be wrong, incomplete, or misread. You must review the original document and confirm every detail before you rely on it.
  • Mendora does not keep the AI-processed content stored long-term on its own servers.
  • OpenAI processes requests under its own policies. Do not submit content you are not comfortable sending for cloud AI processing.

Location

Location is optional and is used only when you tap “find a repairer near you”. Mendora requests While Using the App access, reads an approximate position, rounds it before use, and turns it into a suburb label for the search. If you deny location, you can type a suburb or postcode instead. Mendora does not track your location in the background.

Subscription and Billing

  • Mendora Pro is a yearly auto-renewing subscription that unlocks the AI features.
  • The App Store product ID is mendora_year_pro.
  • Mendora Pro includes up to 200 AI actions per subscription year. Every AI action counts — identifying an appliance, a preliminary assessment, a document or business-card scan, and a repairer search.
  • Pricing, currency, billing, renewal, restore, cancellation, and subscription management are handled by Apple through your App Store account.
  • Mendora checks purchase status to enable access and track your AI action count.

Permissions

  • Camera: to photograph appliances, labels, faults, receipts, invoices, repair documents and business cards.
  • Photos / Files: to import images and PDFs, and optionally to save a generated repair-pack export.
  • Notifications: to deliver warranty-expiry, maintenance / service-due and booked repair-visit reminders.
  • Location (optional, While Using the App): only for “find a repairer near you”, as described above.

Notifications

Reminders are scheduled and delivered on your device by iOS. Tapping a reminder opens the related appliance or schedule in Mendora.

Backup, Import and Sharing

Mendora can export your data as a backup file through the iOS Share Sheet; the backup includes the photos and documents attached to your records. You can import a Mendora backup from your device, and Mendora checks for likely duplicates before merging or replacing. When you share a repair pack or a backup, the app you choose receives the content you share.

Storage

Settings › Storage shows how much space your photos and documents use and lets you remove files that no record points at any more. Deleting an appliance keeps its media in this build; use Storage to clear leftovers.

Data Retention

Local app data remains on your device until you delete records, delete backups, or uninstall the app. Subscription and purchase records may be retained as needed for access, billing support, AI-action quota tracking, and legal compliance.

Safety and Accuracy Disclaimer

Mendora's AI assessment is preliminary guidance only and is not a professional diagnosis. Appliance faults can involve gas, electricity, pressurised water, heat and moving parts. Do not attempt repairs you are not qualified and licensed to do. If Mendora shows a safety warning — or if you notice a gas smell, sparks, burning, smoke, or water damage — stop using the appliance and contact a qualified professional or your emergency services. You remain responsible for the safety of your home and for verifying every detail before acting.

Children's Privacy

Mendora is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Policy Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes are posted on this page with an updated date.
