SipWise Support

Last updated: September 6, 2026

SipWise is a private drink journal. You log what you drink and it shows four separate pictures — hydration, caffeine, free sugar and alcohol. Need help? Contact us at maxadlaistudio@gmail.com.

Common Topics

1) What SipWise Does

  • Four lenses, not one score: hydration, caffeine, free sugar and alcohol are tracked separately, because they mean different things.
  • Today: your running totals for the day, each against its own goal or reference.
  • Your usuals: tap a drink to log it instantly; press and hold to pick a size.
  • Diary: a calendar of what you drank, day by day.
  • Insights: weekly patterns and how this week compares with last.
  • Drink library: the built-in catalogue plus any drinks you add, each with your own default serving.

2) Logging a Drink

  • Tap a usual on the Home screen — one tap logs it.
  • Choose opens your drink library to pick and set a size.
  • Say and Scan use AI and need SipWise Plus (see below).
  • You can backdate a drink to an earlier day from the Diary.

3) SipWise Plus and AI Logging

  • SipWise Plus unlocks two AI ways to log: Say (describe a drink out loud) and Scan (photograph a drink or its label). Everything else in SipWise is free.
  • The AI returns estimated details — drink, size, caffeine, sugar, alcohol. Nothing is logged until you review and confirm it.
  • SipWise Plus is an auto-renewing yearly subscription. App Store product ID: sipwise_ios_pro_year. Pricing, currency, renewal timing and billing are handled by Apple and shown in the purchase sheet.
  • Plus includes an annual allowance of AI logs (currently about 1200 per subscription period; subject to change). Settings shows your usage and reset date. When it runs out, keep logging by tap or by hand until it resets.
  • Tap Restore on the SipWise Plus screen to recover a subscription bought with the same Apple ID.
  • Manage or cancel from your Apple account subscription settings.

4) Logging by Voice

  • Tap Say, allow microphone and speech recognition once, then describe what you drank in a normal sentence.
  • You can name more than one drink at once — “a large latte and a glass of water”.
  • Speech is turned into text by Apple on your device; SipWise sends the text, not a recording.
  • Say the size if you know it (“a 500 ml bottle”); otherwise SipWise estimates and you can adjust before confirming.

5) Logging by Photo or Label

  • Use a clear, straight-on photo with good lighting and no glare.
  • For packaged drinks, include the nutrition label so caffeine and sugar can be read rather than estimated.
  • Review every number against the label before you confirm — from a label they are read, from a plain cup they are estimated.
  • If the photo can't be read, fill the drink in by hand or try a sharper photo. The photo is not stored on our servers.

6) Goals and References

  • Hydration goal: set it yourself, or let SipWise personalise it from your weight and activity.
  • Caffeine reference: a general daily figure you can adjust.
  • Free sugar: shown against a World Health Organization total-diet reference. It counts only the sugar from your logged drinks — it is not a “budget” and going over is not an alarm.
  • Alcohol: awareness only. SipWise shows standard drinks this week and your alcohol-free days, following the Australian guideline. It never shows a “remaining allowance”.
  • Pregnancy / breastfeeding mode: adjusts the caffeine reference and replaces the alcohol count with health guidance.

7) Reminders and Notifications

  • Turn on Water reminders in Settings for a gentle nudge — only if you are behind your goal, in the mid-afternoon, never in the evening, never about alcohol.
  • Add your own reminders under Settings › All reminders — fixed times or check-ins.
  • If alerts do not appear, check iOS notification permission, Focus / Do Not Disturb, and device time settings.

8) Your Data

  • Everything is stored on your device. There is no account and nothing is synced.
  • Settings › Export data saves your journal as JSON or CSV through the Share Sheet. This is free.
  • The database and photos are excluded from iCloud and device backups. Deleting the app deletes the journal — export first.

9) Accuracy and Health

  • SipWise is a journal, not medical advice and not a medical device. Every figure is a general estimate.
  • AI output can be incomplete, inaccurate or misread — always review before confirming.
  • For medical advice, including during pregnancy or breastfeeding, talk to a qualified professional.

10) Appearance

  • Settings › Appearance changes the accent colour (default teal), light / dark / auto theme, and a calmer “calm” style.

When Contacting Support

  • Device model and iOS version.
  • SipWise app version and build number (Settings › About).
  • What happened and what you expected.
  • Screenshots and any error message.
  • If AI-related: whether it happened during voice logging or photo / label logging.
  • For purchase issues: Apple ID region and approximate purchase time.
